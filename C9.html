1# User Frontend Portal for C9

This application serves as the user-facing frontend portal for a telecom platform known as C9. It is designed to provide end-users with an intuitive and responsive interface where they can access telecom-related features and services. The portal allows users to view and manage their telecom accounts, access call history, purchase or manage phone numbers, monitor usage, and interact with support. Built with a modern frontend framework, the portal ensures a seamless experience for telecom customers, bridging the backend infrastructure with user-friendly design.
