Deply

Click to view live

1# Digital Product Passport Platform

Deply is a product and supply-chain platform focused on Digital Product Passports (DPPs), documents, inventory, suppliers, and QR-based product sharing. Companies can list products, raw materials, and inventory, then attach documents and processes to them. They can also manage suppliers, view supplier profiles, and keep supplier-related assessments and contacts in one place.

2# Products, Inventory & Documents

Users can organize products and raw materials with linked inventory records. Documents and process details can be attached directly to items, making it easier to maintain compliance data, product history, and operational records in one workflow.

3# Supplier Management

Deply helps teams manage suppliers in a single place—profiles, contacts, and assessments—so supply-chain collaboration stays structured and easy to track across the product lifecycle.

4# QR Sharing & Consumer App

QR codes let companies share product information quickly. The consumer app lets end users scan and check product details, while billing, team management, and onboarding are handled in the main business app.

5# Two Frontends

The monorepo is split into two frontends:

  • deply-app — merchant/admin app with dashboard, products, documents, inventory, QR generator, billing, and onboarding
  • deply-consumer-app — public consumer app for viewing and checking products

6# Tech Stack

Built with a Next.js + Firebase + Stripe TypeScript stack, separated into a business dashboard and a consumer-facing product passport viewer.
