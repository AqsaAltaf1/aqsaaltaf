#1 Application for Hides (Inspired by Airbnb)

This application is a nature-focused accommodation platform inspired by Airbnb, tailored specifically for unique hideaways such as cabins in the mountains, forest lodges, lakeside retreats, and remote natural spots. Users can browse and book various "hides" (private places to stay), each listed with detailed descriptions, pricing, location, and high-quality images showcasing the natural surroundings. Property owners can also register on the platform to list their own hideaways, including descriptions, availability, amenities, and images to attract travelers.

#2 Booking System

The platform includes a robust and user-friendly booking system. Users can select a hide based on their preferences such as location, type of retreat, price range, and available dates. Discounts may be applied automatically to properties with high ratings or during promotional periods. The booking process includes a calendar view for availability, secure payment integration, and real-time confirmation. This makes it easy for users to plan their escapes to nature with confidence and convenience.

#3 Rating and Review System

After staying at a hide, users can leave a review and rate their experience. Ratings are submitted in the form of stars (typically 1 to 5), and users can also leave detailed written feedback about the property, cleanliness, location, amenities, and host behavior. These reviews help future travelers make informed decisions and also provide valuable feedback to property owners for continuous improvement.
