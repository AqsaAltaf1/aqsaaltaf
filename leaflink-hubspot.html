1# LeafLink → HubSpot Automation

A data pipeline that keeps a HubSpot CRM in step with LeafLink, the wholesale cannabis marketplace. Every order, retailer, buyer and status change in LeafLink is pushed into HubSpot automatically, so the sales team works from live data instead of re-keying records by hand.

2# What Gets Synced

Four LeafLink objects map onto their natural HubSpot counterparts, so the CRM mirrors the marketplace without duplicating anything:

  • Order → Deal — amount, close date and owner populated from the order.
  • Retailer → Company — dispensary details and licence data create the account record.
  • Buyer → Contact — contact name, email and phone, linked to both company and deal.
  • Order status → Deal stage — pending, shipped and delivered move the deal through the pipeline.

3# The Automation Engine

The middle layer runs on Zapier, Make and n8n against the LeafLink and HubSpot APIs, in a three-step cycle:

  • Trigger — fires on a new or updated order.
  • Transform — maps and cleans fields so values match HubSpot's property schema.
  • Upsert — creates the record if it is new, updates it if it already exists.

The upsert step is what keeps the CRM clean: records are matched before they are written, so a repeat sync updates the existing company, contact or deal instead of creating a second copy.

4# End-to-End Flow

  • 1 · Trigger — an order is placed or updated in LeafLink.
  • 2 · Map — fields are matched to the corresponding HubSpot properties.
  • 3 · Sync — company, contact and deal are created or updated together.
  • 4 · Follow up — the deal moves along the pipeline and tasks are assigned to the team.

5# Outcome

  • No manual data entry between the marketplace and the CRM.
  • Real-time sync as orders and statuses change.
  • Zero duplicate records, thanks to match-then-upsert.
  • A CRM that is always current, so reporting and follow-up are based on live order data.
