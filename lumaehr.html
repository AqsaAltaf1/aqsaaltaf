LumaEHR

1# Clinic Practice Management Platform

LumaEHR is an electronic health record and practice management platform that brings everything a clinic runs on into one place: patient records, appointments, billing and day-to-day operations. The product pairs a marketing site with a full application dashboard, so clinics can sign up and start working without a lengthy onboarding process.

Open Live App

2# Patient Records & Appointments

Clinics manage patient records and scheduling from a single dashboard, keeping clinical history, visit information and upcoming appointments linked to each patient rather than spread across separate tools.

3# Billing

Billing is handled inside the same workflow as scheduling and records, so charges stay connected to the appointments and patients they belong to and practices can keep financial and clinical data aligned.

4# Departments & Multi-Tenant Access

The dashboard is organised around an organisation switcher and departments, so a practice can separate teams and areas of the clinic while staff work from one shared account structure. Each organisation keeps its own data, users and settings.

5# Integrations & Settings

A dedicated integrations area lets clinics connect the external services they already use, and a settings area covers account, team and organisation configuration.

6# Marketing Site & Application

The project includes both the public marketing site — pricing, features, resources and help — and the authenticated application behind it, with a free sign-up path and a demo booking flow feeding into the product.
