1# Email Marketing Campaign Management Application

This application is a robust platform designed for executing and managing email marketing campaigns efficiently. It empowers users to create campaigns by uploading targeted email lists, composing customized messages, and sending bulk emails that reach the recipients' inboxes directly. The platform supports every phase of the campaign lifecycle—from planning and scheduling to delivery tracking and analytics—making it an ideal tool for businesses aiming to engage with their audience through email.

2# Email List Upload and Management

Users can upload one or more email lists in supported formats like CSV or Excel. When a list is uploaded, the system automatically checks for and removes duplicate email addresses and validates the syntax of each email to ensure it is properly formatted. Additionally, the system offers advanced filtering options, enabling users to target emails based on patterns such as addresses that start with or end with certain keywords or domains. This intelligent filtering and cleaning process ensures only accurate and relevant emails are included in the campaign, enhancing deliverability and overall effectiveness.

2# Campaign Scheduling and Automation

The application allows campaigns to be scheduled for immediate dispatch or set to go out at a specific future time. This scheduling system is flexible and supports timezone-based delivery, ensuring emails are sent at optimal hours for each recipient group. Campaign drafts are auto-saved and can be reviewed or edited before the scheduled send time. Once a campaign is live, users are notified, making it easy to manage multiple campaigns without missing important timelines.

4# Real-Time Email Tracking and Analytics

The platform integrates with Amazon Web Services Simple Email Service (AWS SES) to provide real-time feedback on each email sent. It tracks whether emails are successfully delivered, bounced, or marked as failed. Furthermore, users can view detailed statistics such as open rates, click-through rates, unsubscribe actions, and spam complaints. These metrics are visualized through easy-to-understand graphs and summaries, offering deep insight into how each campaign performs. This tracking helps businesses analyze recipient engagement and refine future campaigns accordingly.

5# Additional Capabilities

To further enhance campaign effectiveness, the application supports personalized email content using dynamic fields like the recipient’s name or company. It also provides an intuitive drag-and-drop editor for designing custom email templates, allowing even non-technical users to build professional-looking campaigns. Access controls are also built in, enabling different permission levels for team members such as administrators, editors, and viewers. Additionally, the platform supports suppression lists to prevent emails from being sent to invalid or unsubscribed addresses, which protects sender reputation and maintains compliance with email regulations.

6# Stripe Integration

The application is integrated with Stripe to manage user subscriptions securely and efficiently. Users can purchase a subscription plan that determines the number of email recipients they are allowed to target in their campaigns. Depending on the selected plan—such as Basic, Standard, or Premium—the user will have a predefined limit on the number of emails they can send per campaign or per month. This ensures that billing is aligned with usage, and it allows businesses of all sizes to choose a plan that best suits their marketing needs.
