Case study

Phantom Labs

Home / Projects / Phantom Labs Production ERP
Discuss a similar build → All projects →

1# Production Operations Suite

Phantom Labs is an end-to-end production suite for a regulated manufacturing operation. It pulls planning, production, quality control, compliance, warehousing, shipping and finance into one system, so a team can see the state of every job without moving between spreadsheets and separate tools.

2# Executive Overview

The landing dashboard answers one question: where are the bottlenecks, what is ready, and what is at risk. Headline tiles track jobs in production, blocked jobs, items awaiting a second QC signature, units pending compliance release, sale-ready stock, shipments in transit, unpaid orders and a combined risk count — with at-risk figures highlighted so problems surface before they are chased.

3# Production-to-Delivery Pipeline

A single pipeline strip follows stock through every stage of its life — in production, QC pending, for release, sale-ready, in transit and delivered — so anyone can see where volume is sitting and which stage is holding things up.

4# Planning & Scheduling

The planning area covers the daily production plan, the production queue, a planning calendar and run-rate configuration. A schedule summary reports what is running now, what is on the calendar, what has slipped and what is waiting on materials, with a bottleneck and delay summary calling out overdue jobs directly.

5# Production Execution

Jobs move through work orders, material prep, component staging and the production run itself, each job carrying its own identifier, product and due date so the floor and the office are working from the same record.

6# Packaging, QC & Compliance

Packaging and QC sit alongside a dedicated compliance overview. The two-stage QC sign-off and the compliance release queue are tracked as first-class states, which matters in an industry where stock cannot be sold until it has formally cleared both.

7# Warehouse, Sales & Finance

Warehouse, sales and shipping, and finance overviews complete the picture, connecting finished stock to orders and to the money side — including unpaid orders surfaced on the executive dashboard. Access is role-based, and the interface runs in a dark theme with a light-mode toggle.
