1# Application for Online Government Fund Distribution

This application is developed to facilitate the online distribution of government-issued funds to the public. The process begins with the disbursement of funds from central authorities (such as PZA), which are then allocated to various districts. From there, the funds are further distributed to eligible individuals and institutions within those regions. The platform ensures transparency, accountability, and efficient tracking of public welfare funds.

#2 Fund Tracking and Reporting

The system maintains a complete record of funds distributed at every level—starting from PZA to districts, and then down to individual beneficiaries. Allocation is based on population data and regional needs. Detailed reports are generated to show how funds are used across various sectors such as hospitals, madrassas, education, and marriage support programs. This ensures transparency and enables data-driven decision-making.

3# OneLink & Easypaisa Integration

The platform is integrated with OneLink and Easypaisa services for secure and efficient digital payments. With just one click, funds can be disbursed directly to all eligible beneficiaries listed in the system. This integration streamlines the payment process, minimizes delays, and ensures that the right individuals receive the funds in a timely manner.
